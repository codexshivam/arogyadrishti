PRE-AUTHENTICATION PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS

Arogya (Arogya Drishti DPI)

Android Package ID: teamrocket.arogyadrishti.android • GCP Project: teamrocketnp (asia-south1)

Arogya (Arogya Drishti — Vector-Borne Disease Surveillance Digital Public Infrastructure) is developed and operated by Team Rocket (Chandigarh University) in alignment with the National Center for Vector Borne Diseases Control (NCVBDC), Integrated Health Information Platform (IHIP), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

1. Firebase Phone Verification & Authentication Data Usage

When an authorized field health worker (ASHA, ANM, Sanitary Inspector, or Medical Officer) signs in using their mobile phone number (+91) and 6-digit SMS One-Time Password (OTP) via Google Firebase Authentication:

  • The mobile number is used solely to authenticate the officer's identity and verify their assigned ward jurisdiction against the official Staff Directory.
  • Phone numbers and authentication identifiers are never sold, rented, or disclosed to third-party marketers or advertisers.

2. Data Residency & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance (asia-south1 Mumbai)

  • Indian Data Sovereignty: All backend services (Google Cloud Run), databases (Cloud Firestore Native), and object storage buckets (Google Cloud Storage) are strictly pinned to asia-south1 (Mumbai, India).
  • Automated PII Redaction: Breeding-site photographs captured in the field undergo in-memory face and vehicle license plate blurring and EXIF metadata stripping before storage.
  • Zero Citizen PII in Field Logs: Field surveillance logs strictly forbid raw citizen identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, or patient names).

3. Device Permissions (Location & Camera)

  • Location (GPS): Used only while the app is in use to tag 8-digit Local Government Directory (LGD) ward codes, retrieve Google Earth Engine (GEE) satellite climate indices, and compute Google Maps Routes API v2 fogging van dispatch routes.
  • Camera: Used only when an officer initiates an AI breeding-site inspection scan.
